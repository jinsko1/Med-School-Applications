# Teamwork / Leadership Shared Draft

Use for prompts about team roles, collaboration, leadership, and working toward a shared goal.

## Bullet points to hit
- Pick one collaborative example from your work (ASI advocacy, lab trainee mentoring, BCSM Ambassador outreach, or Learning Assistant teamwork).
- Describe the shared problem or goal clearly.
- Explain your specific role and how you worked with others to move the team forward.
- Show what you learned about listening, trust, or coordination.
- Describe the impact the collaboration had on the group or the people served.
- Tie the teamwork lesson back to how you will function on medical teams.

Write the reusable essay core here. Keep school-specific names, programs, and final tailoring in each school's local notes file.
