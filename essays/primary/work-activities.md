# AMCAS Work and Activities

Source: 2027 AMCAS Applicant Guide (`/Users/jinko/Downloads/aamc-2027-amcas-applicant-guide_final_0.pdf`)

## Hard Constraints
- AMCAS allows a maximum of 15 Work/Activities entries.
- AMCAS sends these descriptions to schools as plain text.

## Planning Grid
- Clinical
- Nonclinical service
- Research
- Leadership
- Teaching / mentoring
- Employment
- Awards / publications

## Draft Rule
Each entry should show:
1. What you did.
2. What mattered.
3. What changed in you.
