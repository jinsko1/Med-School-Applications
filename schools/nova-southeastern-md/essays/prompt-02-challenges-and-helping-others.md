## Prompt Text
If applicable, please describe how your experience with challenges has informed your perspective, decision-making, or approach to helping others. You may include reflections on your educational path, life experiences, or other formative events. If not applicable, please type NA.

## Synced Backbone
# Challenge / Adversity Backbone

## Situation
Describe the challenge clearly and briefly.

## Response
- What did you do?
- What tradeoffs or hard decisions did you make?
- Who did you rely on?

## Growth
- What changed in you?
- How will this matter in medicine?

# Service / Underserved Backbone

## Community
Name the population or community served with specificity and respect.

## Role
Explain what you actually did, not just what the organization does.

## Learning
- What did the community teach you?
- What structural issues became visible?
- How did this shift your view of medicine?
