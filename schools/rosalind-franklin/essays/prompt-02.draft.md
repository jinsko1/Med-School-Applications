# Chicago Medical School at Rosalind Franklin University of Medicine and Science - Why Chicago Medical School Draft

Chicago Medical School at Rosalind Franklin University’s commitment to active learning, a student-centered curriculum, and self-directed, lifelong learning is largely the reason I decided to apply. Active learning is well suited for me, as I do not wish to be a passive recipient of whatever an instructor decides to deliver. Instead, I wish to participate in the endeavor of learning itself. I may not always wish to be taught, but I do wish to learn.

I enjoy learning on my own time and often read the literature and write about what I learn on my website, as referenced in my primary application. Through the Distinction in Research program, I hope to continue studying UTI virulence factors, specifically motility, and pursue my interest in pathogenesis while developing the habits of inquiry I hope to carry into academic medicine.
