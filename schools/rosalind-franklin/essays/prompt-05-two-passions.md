## Prompt Text
What are two causes or activities you are most passionate about? How do these passions reflect your values and goals as a future physician?

## Synced Backbone
# Future Goals Backbone

## Near-Term

## Continuity
Connect your future goals to real prior experiences.

# Values / Professionalism Backbone

## Value

## Example
Tell the story that demonstrates the value under pressure.
