# Chicago Medical School at Rosalind Franklin University of Medicine and Science - Chicago and North Chicago Region Draft

Chicago is often discussed in the literature because its healthcare infrastructure serves underserved populations in both urban and suburban forms—though how well it serves these populations requires further discussion. As such, neighborhood-level disparities are rather common, with the literature revealing concrete healthcare access problems, especially in Black and Hispanic neighborhoods, namely pharmacy deserts.

As a student looking toward serving underserved populations as a future physician, opportunities like the Interprofessional Community Clinic are especially meaningful. Getting early experience working with such populations is largely the reason I am interested in the Chicago/North Chicago region, where my medical education would be shaped by learning from communities facing both urban and suburban barriers to care.
