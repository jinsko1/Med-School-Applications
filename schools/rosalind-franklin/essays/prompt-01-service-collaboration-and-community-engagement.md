## Prompt Text
Chicago Medical School values service, interprofessional collaboration, and community engagement. Describe an experience that reflects one or more of these values and how you hope to continue this work during your time in medical school.

## Drafting Frame
This is school-specific and only 150 words. Use one compact example and one forward-looking sentence.

- Strong options: juvenile hall service, hospice, ASI/student-health advocacy, or teaching/mentorship.
- Tie the continued work to CMS's interprofessional/community-facing setting.
