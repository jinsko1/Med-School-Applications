# University of Illinois College of Medicine - Why UICOM Draft

I am mainly interested in UI COM because its curriculum appears geared toward training that would help me become the kind of physician I hope to be. As a future physician, I hope to understand disease within its context; that is, I hope to remain attentive to the social determinants that produce and exacerbate it. UI COM’s commitment to social responsibility and health equity is especially meaningful to me, as my experiences in juvenile hall service, hospice care, student advocacy, and research have taught me that illness is not solely biological.

As such, I hope to take advantage of opportunities like the Urban Medicine Program, whose emphasis on disparities in healthcare access agree with my interests. My service values would consequently be placed into a more concrete, tangible framework, particularly through longitudinal community involvement. I am also greatly interested in the concentration programs, especially Urban Medicine and Rural Medicine, as I hope to practice medicine in underserved communities where need is greatest but care is often sparse.

Lastly, I would like to continue asking clinically pertinent questions through UTI research. UI COM’s research environment would allow me to preserve this habit of curiosity while developing as a physician grounded in service.
