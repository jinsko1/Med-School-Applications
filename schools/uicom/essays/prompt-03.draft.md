# University of Illinois College of Medicine - Two Campuses Draft

Medicine requires the amalgamation of vastly different perspectives and each of the three campuses seem to offer a different set of clinical and community settings.

The stately Chicago campus is interesting because it is the world’s largest medical district, serving diverse, underserved patients. I greatly appreciate the early clinical exposure the students get to take advantage of, learning to take histories in their first year. Education through direct experience and practice agrees with my philosophy of how to learn medicine, as I do not wish to be a passive receiver of drab information.

The Peoria campus is interesting because it is proximate to more than 900 patient beds and a Level 1 trauma center, again, allowing students hands-on experience with caring for patients. I’m particularly interested in the Equity Innovation Medicine Program to learn medicine with its current inadequacies in mind.

Finally, Rockford underscores its rural health education through the National Center for Rural Health Professions and opportunities I am duly interested in like the RMED program. This program would  expose me to another perspective in underserved medicine.

The three campuses collectively reflect different social circumstances, and learning across these realities concomitantly would help me become more attentive to how settings shapes health, a lesson I will surely come back to in a career serving underserved populations.
