## Prompt Text
At MCW, we are guided by the values of acting in caring ways, engaging in collaborative efforts, approaching our world with curiosity, advancing inclusive practices, demonstrating integrity in all that we do, and treating everyone with respect. Describe how your unique experiences, interests, and talents have embodied one or more of these values, and how they will shape the way you plan to contribute to the MCW learning community.

## Drafting Frame
This is an MCW-specific values and contribution prompt. Do not force it into a generic diversity essay.

- Pick one or two MCW values that you can prove through behavior.
- Strong fits: curiosity through research, caring/respect through hospice, inclusive practice through juvenile hall or ASI advocacy, collaborative effort through teaching or lab mentorship.
- End with how those behaviors would shape your contribution to classmates.
