## Prompt Text
Give an example of when you had to work with someone who was difficult to get along with. Why was this person difficult? How did you handle that person?

## Synced Backbone
# Teamwork Backbone

## Team Situation
Pick a team-based example with visible interdependence.

## Your Role
- What role did you play?
- What role did others play?
- How did you adjust?

## Outcome
Show what good teamwork required from you.

# Challenge / Adversity Backbone

## Situation
Describe the challenge clearly and briefly.

## Response
- What did you do?
- What tradeoffs or hard decisions did you make?
- Who did you rely on?

## Growth
- What changed in you?
- How will this matter in medicine?
