## Prompt Text
Describe the last time you were criticized by a peer or supervisor. How did you handle it?

## Synced Backbone
# Challenge / Adversity Backbone

## Situation
Describe the challenge clearly and briefly.

## Response
- What did you do?
- What tradeoffs or hard decisions did you make?
- Who did you rely on?

## Growth
- What changed in you?
- How will this matter in medicine?

# Values / Professionalism Backbone

## Value
Choose one value you can prove with behavior.

## Example
Tell the story that demonstrates the value under pressure.

## Reflection
Explain how that value will show up in medicine.
