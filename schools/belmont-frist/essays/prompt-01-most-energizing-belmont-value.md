## Prompt Text
At FCoM, our mission, vision, and values shape our purpose, set our aspirations, and inform our decisions. Please select which value you find most energizing and why. Discuss how this value has impacted your life. Values: We love learning, Lead by serving, Live with integrity, Heal together in humility, and Welcome difference.
