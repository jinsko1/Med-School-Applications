## Prompt Text
The MD Program includes substantial content in Clinical Public Health, including population health, health systems science, health policy, and community health, to prepare GW graduates for an expanded scope of practice required to be successful 21st century physicians. What are your specific interests and experiences related to that aspect of the MD Program?

## Synced Backbone
# Service / Underserved Backbone

## Community
Name the population or community served with specificity and respect.

## Role
Explain what you actually did, not just what the organization does.

## Learning
- What did the community teach you?
- What structural issues became visible?
- How did this shift your view of medicine?

# Mission Fit Backbone

## School Mission
Name the part of the mission that genuinely overlaps with your path.

## Evidence
Pair each mission point with a concrete experience.

## Future
Explain how that mission fit continues in medical school and beyond.
