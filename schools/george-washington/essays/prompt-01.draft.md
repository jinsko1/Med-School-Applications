# George Washington University School of Medicine & Health Sciences - Regional Medical Campus Interest Draft

The curriculum offered by the Regional Medical Campus appears particularly well suited to the clinical education I hope to receive. As I have stated elsewhere, I am interested in practicing medicine in underserved communities, where illness is often inextricable from social circumstance. A curriculum oriented toward disambiguating these circumstances would greatly benefit my education.

I appreciate the single-site clinical model especially. Repeated and consistent presence matters in a community setting, as it builds familiarity and, consequently, trust. These longitudinal relationships with the surrounding community would allow me to understand the patterns of care that shape a population. The smaller learning environment also interests me, as I can learn through more direct access to mentors.

Disease ought to be understood within its context. As such, a clinical education at the RMC would allow me to continue this habit in a setting structured around community and population health.
