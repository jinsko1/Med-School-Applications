# Kaiser Permanente Bernard J. Tyson School of Medicine - Mission and Values Alignment Draft

I believe my advocacy in student government, research, and community service closely aligns with the virtues extolled by Kaiser.

During my involvement in student government, I coauthored a proposal to successfully install sanitary pads in restrooms of the university union and recreation center, fostering a more inclusive campus for the student body. My term also passed and saw the installation of three health-care product vending machines across campus, allowing products from birth control to analgesics to be available for students.

Further, participating in UTI research has made me aware of disparities in the literature, as I found the literature concerning UTIs in women shamefully sparse. Specifically, on top of the myriad health problems that post-menopausal women face, recurrent urinary tract infections cause significant discomfort and economic burden yet remain understudied, revealing a desperate need for trials that evaluate the treatment and prophylaxis of UTIs in women. This has strengthened my interest in inquiry to address overlooked problems in medicine.

Community service has allowed me to get to know youth in juvenile hall beyond their charges, an experience which ultimately led to the opportunity to share their art through a weekly newsletter sent to volunteers, guardians, and donors. In doing so, I hoped to affirm the youths’ individuality while giving those outside juvenile hall a more human view of the community.

I hope to further this embodiment of inquiry, innovation, and service through my education.
