# California Northstate University College of Medicine - Why CNUCOM Draft

What makes CNUCOM especially attractive to me is, in part, its integrated curriculum. I lean toward the liberal sides of the SPICES model of medical education, and CNUCOM’s use of Clinical Presentations aligns with my views regarding active participation in one’s own education. A curriculum that intertwines basic science, ethics, clinical skills, and patient care into one coherent education is meaningful to me because it remains cognizant of the communities medicine is ultimately meant to serve.

Active learning is well suited for me, as I do not wish to be a passive receiver of whatever an instructor decides to deliver. Instead, I wish to participate in the endeavor of learning itself, as in a student-centered curriculum. I may not always wish to be taught, but I do wish to learn. I enjoy learning on my own time, and will often read the literature and write about what I learn to further consolidate my knowledge on my website, as referenced in my primary application. CNUCOM’s commitment to self-directed, lifelong learning is largely the reason I decided to apply.

Further, as I discuss in my personal statement, I hope to work with underserved communities as a healthcare professional. CNUCOM’s service-learning opportunities are particularly attractive to me, especially Clínica Tepati. As I plan on practicing medicine in California, receiving my education from CNUCOM would help me familiarize myself with the community I hope to serve.

I hope to bring to CNUCOM a willingness to learn actively and serve patiently. In return, I believe CNUCOM would train me to become a physician who embodies scientific rigor and community practice.
