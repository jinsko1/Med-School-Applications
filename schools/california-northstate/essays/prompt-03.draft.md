# California Northstate University College of Medicine - Pre-Health Advisor Experience Draft

Yes, I did have a pre-health advisor whom I frequently relied on throughout my undergraduate studies. Regular meetings with my advisor helped me plan the general timeline for my application, fulfill coursework requirements, and select schools. The guidance provided made the path to a career in medicine clearer, but like any other advising resource, I was responsible for reflecting on my own motivations, considering how my values aligned with the career, and seeking formative experiences in doing so.

Further, I distinctly recall using resources such as the MSAR, admissions webinars, and conversations with local physicians to consolidate my interest in medicine and simply satisfy my curiosity about the subject.

So although I did have a pre-health advisor, much of the guidance I received merely oriented me to the process, and I was largely responsible for the mechanics involved in preparing for a career in medicine.
