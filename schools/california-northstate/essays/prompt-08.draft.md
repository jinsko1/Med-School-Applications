# California Northstate University College of Medicine - Standardized Tests and MCAT Preparation Draft

I am generally wary of arguments that extol standardized tests as accurate metrics of competency, especially the MCAT. I prepared independently with review textbooks, flashcards, practice questions, and full-length exams.

I do not believe the MCAT alone can predict how competent I will become as a physician; memorizing facts to regurgitate them does little good without judgment, humility, and etc. However, the exam required me to familiarize myself with a large body of material and reason under time constraints. As such, my score reflects not my full identity as a future physician, but my ability to learn.

From what little I know of the USMLE, since I assume standardized tests to be roughly similar in nature and preparation, I believe the MCAT has prepared me well. The habits, not the score, should extend into preparing for the USMLE.
