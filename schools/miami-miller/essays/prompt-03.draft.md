# University of Miami Leonard M. Miller School of Medicine - Values Draft

Of the MedCanes values presented, I believe I exemplify accountability the most.

During my service in student government, I coauthored a proposal to successfully install sanitary pads in restrooms of the university union and recreation center, fostering a more inclusive campus for the student body. My term also passed and saw the installation of three healthcare product vending machines across campus, allowing products from birth control to analgesics to be available for students. In essence, both accomplishments resulted from identifying these issues through student interaction and taking initiative to act on them—qualities that exemplify the core value.

Further, outside of my community service in juvenile hall, I believe participating in hospice care has cultivated these core values as well. With my hospice client expressing clear reluctance toward the therapy exercises her daughter wished her to complete, I often found myself in a quagmire as to what to do, as my client often cooped herself up in the restroom, refusing to come out. Rather than persisting through repeated knocking and placating—which must have been an imposition—I gradually waited patiently and allowed her to come out on her own terms, after which I completed the exercises alongside her, sharing the burden rather than merely making her endure them alone.

I believe these experiences have nurtured my accountability, a value sought after in healthcare, and I hope to further them during my medical education.
