# Eastern Virginia Medical School - Exposure to Medicine Draft

Much like the microscopic undulations on his wrist, his heartbeat kept pace with the ball being dribbled across the court. I told him that’s where the arteries were, beneath the correctional uniform. He was not allowed to exert himself; there was sugar in his blood.

Blood.

It reminded me of childhood, when I first learned of blood. Grandmother taught me that if I rested my hand over the flashlight, my hand would turn red. Later, my mother cooked for her on account of the sugar in her blood. It was not as if my mother had much time; she took time off from teaching to care for grandmother. She did not explain compassion as an idea. She practiced it while tired, busy, and inconvenienced, yet made it look ordinary.

As such, I came to understand that illness did not only belong to the person afflicted, but to the whole household. It shaped what was cooked, how adults spoke in lowered voices, and what a child could understand only through fragments.

By middle school, nascent compassion had extended its influence into curiosity. I remember picking up Medical Detectives, delighted by the elegance of how the cases were solved. I was absorbed by the way each piece of a medical mystery fit together, each discipline contributing a different clue.

Through my work on UTIs, I learned to ask meaningful questions, return repeatedly to the literature, and develop patience. Service gave it direction. In juvenile hall, compassion was not merely an abstract concept: it meant showing up consistently, remaining present with another person’s fear or pain.

By degrees, latent compassion became curiosity; and curiosity became research, service, and action. At the intersection of all these commitments: medicine. I hope to practice medicine with the curiosity that began when I first watched light pass through my hand, and the compassion learnt from my tired mother. Only later did I understand that sweetness was never in the blood itself, but in the quiet acts of care that surrounded it.
