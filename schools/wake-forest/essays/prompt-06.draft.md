# Wake Forest University School of Medicine - Why Wake Forest Draft

 Quality medical education should not consist of the passive accumulation of facts before encountering real human interaction. Students should learn through patients and communities, actively engaging in problems. Wake Forest’s two campus model appeals to me because they treat students as active participants in their own education, and allow patient interaction early on.

I am also drawn to the Medical Student Research Program because my years in microbiology research have taught me that concentrated research time changes how deeply a student can engage with a field. Summer research gave me more time to think and truly understand what I was doing in the lab. Finally, the Delivering Equal Access to Care Clinic reflects the kind of community-based training I desire. My work with underserved populations in juvenile hall has deepened my desire to pursue a medical education rooted in service to such communities.
