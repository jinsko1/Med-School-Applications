## Prompt Text
The mission of our academic health system is to improve health for all. Consider your abilities, insights, and previous experiences and then tell us about your potential to help improve health for all.

## Synced Backbone
# Mission Fit Backbone

## Mission Match
Name the mission value or institutional priority you genuinely match.

## Evidence
- What have you already done that demonstrates this fit?
- What patient population, setting, or problem did you engage?
- What did the experience teach you about medicine?

## Forward Link
Explain how you would carry this pattern into the school community and future patient care.

# Service Backbone

## Service Setting
Describe the community, patient population, or person served.

## Your Role
- What did you actually do?
- What responsibility did you hold?
- What did the experience require from you emotionally or practically?

## Impact and Growth
Show what changed for the people served and what changed in how you understand medicine.
