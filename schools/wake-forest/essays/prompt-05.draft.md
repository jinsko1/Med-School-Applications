# Wake Forest University School of Medicine - Commitment Draft

_Not linked to a shared essay draft: this prompt asks for a specific commitment story rather than a generic future-goals essay._

## Bullet points to hit
- Choose a sustained commitment with visible follow-through (longitudinal research, hospice work, Restorative Partners, ASI advocacy, or Learning Assistant teaching).
- Describe what you committed to and how long it lasted.
- Explain what made staying committed difficult or slow.
- Show what you did when the work became inconvenient or emotionally taxing.
- Highlight the value or mindset the commitment reveals about you.
- Tie that commitment to how you will approach patients or medical training.
