# Wayne State University School of Medicine - Healthcare Challenges in Under-Resourced Communities Draft

My experience working with youth in juvenile hall—many of whom come from under-resourced communities—has brought much of my attention to ACEs: adverse childhood experiences.

There is no simple explanation for such harmful factors in healthcare, but ACEs are, broadly speaking, begotten by poverty, unsafe housing, community violence, family instability, underfunded schools, and other social conditions. Unfortunately, far too many variables are involved, and condensing these social factors would be forcefully pithy and reductive. That being said, one major failure of current healthcare practice lies in the treatment of downstream manifestations, such as depression, rather than the exploration of upstream factors: what causes them.

Knowing this, as a future physician, I must remember not only to treat the manifestations of ACEs, but also to advocate for healthcare that deals with their root causes. If a physician fails to recognize this, they risk treating only the visible consequences rather than the conditions that produced them.
