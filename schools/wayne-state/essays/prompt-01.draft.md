# Wayne State University School of Medicine - Motivation and Challenges of Medicine Draft

Outside of what has already been said in my primary application, a field which is perpetually in flux such as medicine requires lifelong learning. This entails continuous identification of the gaps in one’s knowledge—which requires humility before one’s own ignorance—fulfillment of those gaps through self-directed education, and constant vigilance for new and contradictory knowledge in the literature.

This commitment—which I consider an ethical obligation to patients—I find intellectually rewarding and noble beyond any other pursuit. The resilience and tenacity I observe in my local physicians to remain by their core values have been chief reasons, motivations for a career in medicine. But I also give assent that there are opposing forces that may challenge one’s medical integrity.

When interacting with patients who have lived vastly different lives from our own, we must consciously resist the urge to form biases, for not doing so would represent the failure of providing quality medical care and reject belonging, the ultimate form of negligence.

I find the constant reflection and refinement of one’s own practice a noble pursuit, and therefore has motivated my interest in medicine.
