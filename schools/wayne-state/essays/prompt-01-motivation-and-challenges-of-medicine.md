## Prompt Text
When reflecting on the roles and responsibilities of a physician, please discuss what motivated your interest. What are potential challenges to pursuing a career in medicine?

## Synced Backbone
# Future Goals Backbone

## Near-Term
What do you hope to learn or become in medical school?

## Longer-Term
What kind of physician or leader are you building toward?

## Continuity
Connect your future goals to real prior experiences.

# Challenge / Adversity Backbone

## Situation
Describe the challenge clearly and briefly.

## Response
- What did you do?
- What tradeoffs or hard decisions did you make?
- Who did you rely on?

## Growth
- What changed in you?
- How will this matter in medicine?
