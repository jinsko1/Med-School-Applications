## Prompt Text
Please answer if you have had a gap or growth year (career change, re-applicant, additional education, etc.). A growth year is defined as a period of time between the end of your undergraduate education and a year or more prior to the start of medical school. What activities have you participated in or plan to participate in during this period? How does this relate to your future career in medicine?
