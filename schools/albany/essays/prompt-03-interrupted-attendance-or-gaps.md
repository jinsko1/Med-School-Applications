## Prompt Text
Has your college or university, graduate or professional school attendance been interrupted for any reason? If yes, please explain. Also, please explain any extended gaps in activity/employment in your post-graduate history. If the question does not apply to you, please put N/A in the box provided.

## Synced Backbone
# Gap Year / Current Year Backbone

## Timeline
List work, service, research, coursework, and personal commitments in order.

## Purpose
Explain why this year matters for your development, not just what you are doing.

## Connection to Medicine
- Skills built
- Exposure deepened
- Perspective gained

# Academic Context Backbone

## Context
State the issue plainly: low grades, withdrawals, extended timeline, or a break.

## Accountability
Own what is yours without overexplaining.

## Recovery
- What changed?
- What evidence shows improvement?
- What habits or systems are now different?
