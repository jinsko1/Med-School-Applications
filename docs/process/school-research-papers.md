# School Research Papers

Publication blurbs on each school dashboard come from `data/school_research_papers.json`.

## Source
- PubMed / NCBI E-utilities.
- Searches use school-affiliation terms plus applicant-relevant topics: health disparities, community health, underserved care, medical education, clinical care, infection, microbiology, women's health, public health, and health equity.
- Results are limited to recent publications from 2021 onward.

## Caveat
This is a fast school-fit research layer, not a full literature review. For newer schools, affiliation data can be sparse or broad because faculty may publish under hospitals, partner universities, or prior affiliations.

Before citing a paper in an actual secondary, open the PubMed link and verify the authors, affiliation, and topic fit.
