# School Research Papers

Publication blurbs on each school dashboard come from `data/school_research_papers.json`.

## Source
- PubMed / NCBI E-utilities.
- Searches use school-affiliation terms plus applicant-relevant topics: microbiology, infectious disease, antimicrobial resistance, women's health, underserved care, health equity, hospice/palliative care, and major clinical/scientific publications.
- The fetcher keeps at most one paper per school.
- Weak matches are intentionally left blank instead of padded with filler.
- Several schools use manual selections when a better paper is clearly more useful for essay writing than the top automated result.

## Caveat
This is a fast school-fit research layer, not a full literature review. For newer schools, affiliation data can be sparse or broad because faculty may publish under hospitals, partner universities, or prior affiliations.

Before citing a paper in an actual secondary, open the PubMed link and verify the authors, affiliation, and topic fit.
