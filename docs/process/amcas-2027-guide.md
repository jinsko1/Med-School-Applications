# AMCAS 2027 Guide Notes

Primary source: `/Users/jinko/Downloads/aamc-2027-amcas-applicant-guide_final_0.pdf`

## Verified Notes From The Guide
- AMCAS personal comments essay: 5,300 characters including spaces.
- AMCAS verification may take six to eight weeks during peak season.
- Regular MD applicants must have official transcripts received within 14 calendar days of each school's AMCAS deadline.
- Letters are not required for AMCAS verification, but they are transmitted to schools as AMCAS receives them.
- AMCAS allows a maximum of 15 Work/Activities entries.

## Operational Takeaways
- Submit AMCAS as early as your primary is truly ready; verification timing is the main bottleneck.
- Track transcript arrival separately from letter arrival.
- Draft secondaries before invitations land, especially for your highest-priority schools.
- Preserve reusable language in shared draft sources, then customize final school drafts directly.
