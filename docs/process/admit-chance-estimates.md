# Admit-Chance Estimates

The percentages shown in the review index are heuristic planning estimates, not true admissions probabilities.

## Assumptions
- California resident.
- GPA assumption: 3.75.
- MCAT assumption: 517.
- Strong fit profile: research, clinical exposure, sustained service, student advocacy, teaching, leadership, and scientific communication.

## Inputs
- Cached 2027 MSAR-style applicant, interview, and matriculant counts by residency category where available.
- Cached accepted-student GPA and MCAT medians.
- Public/private and in-state/out-of-state context.
- Mission fit inferred from prompts, selection language, and your work/activities.

## How To Use
Use the percentage as a sorting and prioritization signal. A `12%` school is not a promise; it means the school is comparatively more favorable than a `3%` school under the assumptions above.

If your final GPA or MCAT differs from these assumptions, rerun or edit `scripts/enrich_school_metadata.py`.
